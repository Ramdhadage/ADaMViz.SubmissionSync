FEATURE 10 · CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

Correction revision traceability

A closed parent keeps its status and decisions. The linked successor receives new artifacts, execution evidence, and review decisions.

History lists IDs, numbers, statuses, and times. Parent links and correction text are retained in repository records. Inter and Geist Mono load from Google Fonts; Segoe UI, Arial, and Consolas provide offline fallbacks.
