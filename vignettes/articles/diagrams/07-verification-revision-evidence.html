FEATURE 07 · CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION

Execution verification and revision evidence

The execution service records checks before accepting artifacts and promoting a revision. The repository promotion method can also be called directly; it checks status and version but does not require passing evidence.

Read the feature documentation. Six nodes summarize the service path; context fields, individual checks, and recovery limits are detailed in the documentation. Application colors and Inter typography are retained; offline fallback fonts are Segoe UI/Arial and Consolas.
