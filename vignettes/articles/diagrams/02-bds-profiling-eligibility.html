BDS profiling and plotting eligibility

A selected-data compatibility check precedes generation. Passing the profile preserves warnings and does not establish ADaM conformance.

Reading order: missing subjects and treatment values are checked after parameter/unit selection, before treatment/visit exclusions. Visit mapping and duplicate-key checks follow those exclusions. This overview groups checks; see the feature documentation for exact ordering.

Style follows the application palette. Google Fonts supplies Inter and Geist Mono; Segoe UI or Arial and Consolas remain offline fallbacks.
